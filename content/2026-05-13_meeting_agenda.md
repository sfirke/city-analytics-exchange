Next week we'll be demoing + reacting to **AI in practice**.

We'll have a few presenters give an informal talk about how they're using AI. Each will be followed by Q&A / discussion.
 
Presenters will cover whatever they want from this list:
- Applications of AI in local government: how are you now applying / do you plan to apply AI to improve services?
- What data + privileges do you give to an LLM (and what's don't you)?
- How do you mitigate risk?
- Lessons learned : what’s worked, what hasn’t, what’s been surprising
- What do you envision next?

Want to participate? Join the [mailing list](/pages/join) as we'll use that to send out the meeting appointment and join link.
