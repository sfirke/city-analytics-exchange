Our July meeting is pushed back a week this year, to July 17th @ 12pm ET / 9am PT. We will be talking **knowledge management**. How do you and your colleagues capture and share information?

This is a discussion call. No presenters. We'll self-select into two groups:

- One focused on **technical artifacts** (git repositories, packaging, componentizing code)
- One focused on **general knowledge practices** (SOPs, documentation, workflows)

We're gathering ideas and discussion prompts for each breakout room. Join the discussion in [our Slack](https://cityanalyticsexchange.slack.com/) to share your thoughts and suggestions. *Note: if your city's domain name needs to be added to the allowlist for Slack, ping Sam*.

Want to participate in future meetings? Join the [mailing list](/pages/join) as we'll use that to send out meeting appointments and the meeting join link. *This month's mailing already went out so ping Sam or hop on Slack to get the July invite*.
