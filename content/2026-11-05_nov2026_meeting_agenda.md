This month's meeting will be a two-part discussion, to be driven by the flow of the conversation.
 
Our two topics are:
 
### What Works Cities certification
 
Does your city have WWC certification or are you considering it? Let’s talk about what that entails and how you approach it. If you are unfamiliar with WWC, start here: https://whatworkscities.bloomberg.org/assessment/
 
### Hackathons / Open Data Dives
 
Has your city organized one? Have you ever participated in one? We'll share lessons learned, advice, and ideas.
 
Join the discussion in [our Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/cityanalyticsexchange/shared_invite/zt-48tfbt6kn-PJ8qbwN8EnQ8PyN~8j_7Bw) to share your thoughts and suggestions. *Note: if your city's domain name needs to be added to the allowlist for Slack, ping Sam on LinkedIn or elsewhere*.
